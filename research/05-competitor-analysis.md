# Competitor Analysis Research

**BRD relevance:** §2 (Competitive Positioning Matrix), §3 (differentiators)

**Key finding up front:** The market validates the BRD's thesis — tools are splitting into two camps: **social acquisition** (ManyChat et al.) and **e-commerce support** (Gorgias et al.). Nobody in the SMB space does both deeply with multi-agent orchestration + Banglish localization. That gap is the product.

## 2. Comment-to-DM Tool Landscape (2026) — direct FR-01/FR-02 benchmark set

| Tool | Free plan | Starting paid | Pricing model | Platforms | Partner status |

| ReplyRush | 1,500 DMs/mo | $10/mo (Lite, 7,500 DMs) | Flat, DM-volume | IG + FB | Official Meta Business Partner |

| Inrō | 100 contacts, 50 AI credits | €12.99/mo (Pro) | Per-active-contact | IG only | Meta Tech Provider |
| LinkDM | 1,000 DMs/mo | $19/mo (Pro, 25k) | Flat, DM-volume | IG + FB | Meta Business Partner |
| CreatorFlow | 500 DMs/mo | $15/mo (Pro, 5k) | Flat, DM-volume | IG only | Official Meta Graph API partner |
| InstantDM | trial only | ~$9–10/mo | Flat, unlimited | IG only | Meta-compliant |

### Landscape implications
- **Pricing model matters**: per-contact billing (ManyChat, Inrō) spikes after viral content; flat/DM-volume (ReplyRush, LinkDM, CreatorFlow) is predictable. Gorgias moved to per-resolved-interaction. **Recommendation for this product: flat volume tiers + per-resolution or per-DM pricing**, not per-contact.
- Most tools send **one pre-written reply**; only Inrō does conversational AI qualification in-DM. An LLM-driven, context-aware comment-to-DM response (personalized to the comment + product) is a differentiator.
- Meta partner certification (Official Meta Business Partner / Tech Provider / Graph API partner) is a defensible trust marker the BRD should claim as a goal.

## 3. What the 2026 market tells us about the BRD product strategy

1. **"Convert a comment into a sale" is THE winning motion** — Lego's "Ralph" bot drove 25% of all social sales through automated gift-finding; ~40% of e-commerce traffic now originates from social platforms (Stormy AI, 2026).
2. **Agentic AI is the current inflection**: bots moved from static button menus → NLP with omnichannel context → goal-oriented discovery & conversion (ManyChat "Agentic AI", Zowie). The BRD's multi-agent orchestration is directionally correct but should be framed as *intent-driven discovery/qualification* in marketing, not just ticket routing.
3. **Two-tool stack is the incumbent norm** (ManyChat for acquisition + Gorgias for support). A single platform doing both is the wedge.
4. **Benchmarks to plan against (2026)**:
   - Containment rate (AI-resolved without human): 75–90%
   - Bot CSAT target: 4.5+/5.0
   - Conversion from DM: ~12.3%
   - Abandoned-cart recovery via proactive chat: ~30–35%
5. **Human-in-the-loop is mandatory**, both by Meta policy and consumer preference (51% of consumers prefer bots for immediate service; 56% expect natural conversation by 2026). The BRD's Human Handoff (FR-08) is not optional — it is a compliance requirement.
